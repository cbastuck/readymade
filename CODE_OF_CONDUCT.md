# Code of Conduct

Two different things live in this file, and they are not the same kind of thing.

**Part 1 is a rule.** It binds participation in the project's own spaces, and we
enforce it.

**Part 2 is a wish.** It is not a rule, not a licence condition, and not
something we can or will enforce. Please don't read it as one.

---

## Part 1 — How we treat each other here

This applies to the project's spaces: issues, pull requests, code review,
discussions, and any other channel the project runs.

Don't:

- harass, bully, threaten, or stalk anyone, or encourage others to;
- attack people for who they are — this includes slurs and demeaning remarks
  about ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual
  orientation, disability, age, or appearance;
- publish someone's personal information without their consent (doxxing), or
  threaten to;
- impersonate another person or organisation, or misrepresent who you speak for;
- post sexual content, and never anything that sexualises minors;
- spam, flood, or derail discussions, or use project spaces to advertise things
  unrelated to the project.

Do assume the person on the other side is trying to build something and may be
having a worse day than you are. Disagreement is fine and useful; contempt is
not. Criticise the code, the design, the argument — not the person.

### If something goes wrong

Report it to christoph@bstck.berlin. Reports are handled confidentially, and you
will not be penalised for making one in good faith.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close threads, and — for repeated
or serious behaviour — block accounts from the project's spaces. We aim to pick
the mildest measure that ends the problem, and to say why we acted. Behaviour
outside these spaces is not our business, unless it's directed at someone
because of their participation here.

Conduct on the hosted services at readymadeit.com is a separate matter, governed
by the [Terms of Use](https://readymadeit.com/terms) — that is a contract; this
file is not.

---

## Part 2 — What we hope you build

Readymade is licensed under AGPL-3.0. That licence gives you the right to run it
for any purpose whatsoever, and this section takes nothing back: it grants no
rights, removes none, and adds no condition to the licence or to any contract.

It also isn't a policy we could enforce if we wanted to. Boards run on your
machines. There is no telemetry, no screening, no remote switch, and we have no
idea what you build. So what follows is genuinely a wish, and nothing more.

We'd like Readymade to be used for things that treat the people on the other
side of them as people: tools that ask before they collect, that let someone see
and correct what's held about them, that fail in ways a human can notice and
undo. Small, personal, useful things. Things that make somebody's day less
tedious.

We'd rather it not be used to watch people who haven't agreed to be watched, to
send messages nobody asked for at a scale nobody can escape, to decide things
about people's lives with no way to contest the decision, or to make someone
believe they are talking to a person when they are talking to a machine.

If you're building something near that second list and it's a genuinely close
call, we'd rather you asked than didn't: christoph@bstck.berlin. Asking costs
you nothing — there is no permission to withhold.
