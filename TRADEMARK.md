# Readymade Name & Branding

The Readymade source code is open source under AGPL-3.0 — you're free
to use, modify, fork, and self-host it, including for commercial
purposes, under the terms of that license.

The name "Readymade" and associated logos are owned by Christoph
Bastuck (BSTCK BRLN) and are separate from the code license — they are
not covered by AGPL. In short:

- You're welcome to say your project "is built on Readymade" or "is a
  fork of Readymade."
- Please don't use the "Readymade" name or logo as the primary brand
  of a competing hosted product/service without checking with us
  first — this is just to avoid user confusion about who they're
  actually buying from.

This is an informal policy for now, not a registered trademark. It may
be formalized later as the project grows. If you have questions about
a specific use, open an issue or reach out.
